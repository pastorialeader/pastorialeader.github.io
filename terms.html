Purpose

EcoFlow Monitor is an independent personal project for viewing local power-station readings and configuring optional notifications. This public website provides project information only; it does not offer a hosted monitoring or messaging service to the public.

Readings, estimates and delivery

Power-source detection is inferred from telemetry. Runtime values are estimates based on current load and stated capacity/loss assumptions. Readings may be delayed, stale or unavailable. Notifications depend on connectivity, third-party services and recipient settings; delivery is not guaranteed. The application is not a substitute for equipment's built-in protections.

Authorized use

Use integrations only with accounts and equipment you are authorized to access. Keep credentials private and follow applicable third-party service terms. The public website does not grant access to the owner's dashboard, devices, private source repository or accounts.

Availability and affiliation

The project is provided as available, without a commitment to uninterrupted operation or support. Features may change. EcoFlow, Google, Gmail, Pushover and GitHub are names of their respective owners; this project is not affiliated with or endorsed by those providers.
